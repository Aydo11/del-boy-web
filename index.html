How it works

Preview wording — steps to be confirmed against the current app.

If you're looking for work

  1. 1

    Browse jobs

    Open DEL-BOY® and look through the quick jobs on offer.

  2. 2

    Check the details

    Tap a job to see what's involved and whether it suits you.

  3. 3

    Take the next step

    Follow the prompts in the app to go ahead.

If you need someone for a job

  1. 1

    Describe the work

    Say what you need doing in a few words.

  2. 2

    Add the details

    Fill in the information the app asks for.

  3. 3

    Follow up in the app

    Keep track of your job from your account.
