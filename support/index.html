Frequently asked questions

Getting started

How do I download DEL-BOY® on iPhone?

DEL-BOY® is launching on the App Store soon. When it's live, use the App Store badge on our homepage or search for DEL-BOY® in the App Store on your iPhone.

Is DEL-BOY® available on Android?

Android is coming soon.

Who is DEL-BOY® for?

People looking for quick jobs, and people who need someone for a job.

Your account

I can't sign in. What should I do?

Check you're using the same details you signed up with. If you're still stuck, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address linked to your account and ask for it to be deleted. See our Privacy notice for what happens to your data.

Jobs

Can I use DEL-BOY® to post a job?

Yes. DEL-BOY® is for people who need someone for a job as well as people looking for work.

Where do I see a job's details?

Tap any job in the app to open its details.

Technical issues

The app isn't working properly.

Make sure you have the latest version of DEL-BOY® and of iOS, then close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.
